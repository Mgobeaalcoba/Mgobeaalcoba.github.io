Before we start

Let’s make it clear.

I have a problem, but I don’t know which technology I need.

That is a good place to start. Tell us what is taking your time or what you want to build. We understand the context first, then recommend automation, AI, development or a combination.

How is a project priced?

Custom projects receive a written quote after we define the scope. You can also start with a diagnostic, audit or mentoring session with published pricing in On-demand services. The diagnostic is a paid service; the applicable fee is credited if we proceed with implementation.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Yes. We review your systems, access and APIs before proposing changes. We aim to integrate what already works and agree on dependencies, third-party costs and limitations upfront.

What happens after delivery?

Delivery includes the agreed documentation and knowledge transfer. We define who operates the system and what support or maintenance it needs. That support is detailed in the proposal.

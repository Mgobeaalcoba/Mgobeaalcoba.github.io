Before you buy

Frequently asked questions.

Are these prices final?

They are reference prices in USD for the published scope. Custom services (agentification and AI agents) are quoted in writing based on scope. Payment is charged in the method and currency shown when you hire.

What if I need an implementation?

The diagnostic produces a scope and a budget. If we move forward, the applicable diagnostic fee is credited toward the project.

Do you only work with companies in Argentina?

No. Sessions and deliverables are remote and can be purchased from any country compatible with the agreed payment method.

When do we schedule the session?

After payment you complete a short onboarding. Scheduling is confirmed within the next 24 hours.
